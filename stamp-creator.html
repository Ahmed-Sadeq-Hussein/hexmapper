Click Load Tile Folder and pick your tile pack (e.g. Assets - 72 DPI (VTT)).

Loading is additive: you can add more folders on top.

Your own images don't need a folder: use Import Image on the right.

1 · Shape

A piece covers one or more hexes. Use Shape to click or drag hexes in and out of the piece. Painting a hex adds it too.

2 · Fill

Your image is added to the active layer and fitted to the piece's shape. Use a PNG with transparency for art that should sit on top of terrain.

Selected

Drag moves · scroll resizes · R rotates 15° · arrows nudge · Del deletes · double-click resets.

3 · Check the Fit

Around the piece: none

4 · Save

Saved pieces show up in the map tool's Stamps panel. Pack tiles are stored by name (so HD renders use HD tiles); your own images are stored inside the piece.

Library

Click a piece to edit it. Saving with the same name updates it; a new name saves a copy.

Asset Sizing & Workspace

Draws every pack asset at the size its artist made it (towns stay town-sized), same as the map tool.
