Tool

Recenter: click a hex & drag (or arrow-keys) to nudge an off-centre tile; double-click resets it. Pan: Pan tool, middle-mouse, hold Space+drag, or scroll. Ctrl+scroll zooms.

River/Road Brush: drag across hexes and the matching river or road pieces are picked and turned for you. Drawing into an existing river makes a junction. Right-drag erases. Hold Shift when you let go to end a river in a lake instead of a spring; ending next to water makes a river mouth.

Flip piece / Flip line: click a wall or cliff to turn it the other way round (a cliff swaps its high and low side; line = every piece connected to it). Delete piece / Delete line: click one to remove it, or the whole connected line. Surround: click a hex to ring its connected area of that tile type with the current wall or cliff, high on the inside. Shift+click rings every area of that type; right-click removes the ring.

Wall Brush: drag along the hex edges to build a wall; corners join up by themselves. Click an edge to add or remove a single wall. Right-drag erases. Load Dungeondraft.pck (in the Dungeondraft install folder) or any .dungeondraft_pack for textures; they are remembered in this browser. White keeps a texture's own colours; any other colour tints it.

Cliff Brush: drag along the hex edges; the ground drops on the right of the way you drag (ticks show the low side). Click an edge to add or remove one piece: the side you click is the high side. Right-drag erases. The cliff colours come from the loaded tiles.

Ctrl+Z undo · Alt+click = eyedropper (copy a hex's terrain)

Active Layer

Terrain replaces the hex. River/Path & Feature stack on top. Picking a river or path tile switches here automatically.

Grid

Towns, banners and other extras keep their true size next to the terrain (same as the Stamp Creator) instead of stretching across the hex.

Stamps

Pieces made in the piece creator show up here. Click one, then click the map to place it.

R turns the piece 60° (Shift+R back), M mirrors it. Rivers and paths turn with it; terrain and buildings stay upright.

How to use

      

        Load the tile folder + your map template. Size the grid to match. Pick a terrain tile, click hexes to lay land.
        Switch to a River tile and draw along valleys — connecting pieces drop on neighbours automatically.
        Right-click erases. Pan with the Pan tool / middle-mouse / Space+drag / scroll.
        Zoom with the on-screen − % + controls, Ctrl+scroll (zooms to cursor), +/−, or 0 to fit the whole map.
        v2: viewport culling + a colour overview when zoomed out, diff-based undo, and compressed saves — built for very large maps.

Foundry VTT (Whole Map / Crop Box)

      
Saves the crop selection (or whole map) as an image + a ready-to-import scene JSON. Foundry > Scenes > Import Data — grid lines up 1:1 (size = hex height).

      
      
      
.dd2vtt needs a crop selection; the image is baked in for the Universal Battlemap Import module. It imports as a square grid at the hex-height size — flip to Hex Columns (Odd) after and it aligns.
